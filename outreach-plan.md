# After the portfolio: get in front of the right person

This is the execution plan **once the public portfolio is finished**. It is not a spray of mail. It is how a remote European instructional designer is actually noticed. The December calendar, accounts, and employer watch list are in [interview-plan.md](interview-plan.md).

**The best option:** be findable, then write to **named people about named roles**, with **one live artefact** in each note. Eight to twelve human touches a week beat five hundred identical emails.

**How a hiring manager will read a blast:** spam. In the EU it also collides with GDPR. “Portfolio in every company inbox” would make you look junior and careless, not senior. Do not buy a list. Do not scrape addresses. Do not send the same letter to every L&D mailbox.

Two avenues, both required:

| Avenue | What it is | Who you are talking to |
|--------|------------|-------------------------|
| **01 Be findable** | They search, you appear | Recruiters and hiring managers who already have a vacancy |
| **02 Be specific** | You walk up to the right desk | A named hiring manager or ID / L&D lead at a company that hires this work |

---

## Gate — do not start volume until these are true

1. The live site URL is on [cv.md](cv.md), [cover-letter.md](cover-letter.md), LinkedIn, and your email signature. It is still `[TO CONFIRM: portfolio URL]`.
2. A phone number is on the CV. Still `[TO CONFIRM: phone]`.
3. A LinkedIn profile exists, headline matches the CV title, and the URL is known. Still `[TO CONFIRM: LinkedIn URL]`.
4. **Finished** for this attack means a hiring manager can open Work and see: job aid, stakeholder deck, onboarding sample, **and** a live Rise module. Storyline if you are applying to Senior ID ads that name it. SCORM Cloud dummy at least. Do not open the volume while Rise is still “in build.”

Until that gate: finish the modules. You may set up LinkedIn and alerts now. You may not send fifty letters.

Search titles (from the 12-week course): Instructional Designer, eLearning Developer, Learning Experience Designer, Digital Learning Designer. Use **L&D Manager** only where the advert is design-heavy or player-coach. That is the later path, not the first wave.

How people in this industry actually sit and get paid: [how-industry-works.md](how-industry-works.md). **Default package: employee on a monthly salary**, fully remote. Contractor invoice only when the advert asks. Not piece work. Days in an office are not a search: no employer in Seville, Córdoba, or Huelva met the role and language criteria on 4 October 2026.

## 01 — Be findable (they come to you)

Hiring managers for these roles look in three places: **LinkedIn**, a **live URL**, and **applications already in their ATS**. They do not hunt random PDFs in a mass inbox.

### LinkedIn (this is the visibility channel)

- Headline: the CV title, not “Director of Studies / artist / software.” Example shape: `Learning Experience Designer | Instructional Designer | Training Programme Director`.
- Location: Seville, open to EU remote. Say EU work authorised.
- About: the professional profile paragraph from the CV. Short. No tax story in the first line.
- Featured: the live site, then **one** artefact (the job aid PDF or the Rise module once it exists). Not eight files.
- Experience: Universal English, Ardmore, Teachertrainingvideos.com, Paris ESP, design years — same facts as [cv.md](cv.md). Do not invent.
- Open to work: recruiters **and** the titles above. Remote, EU.
- Photo: the front-page turtleneck. Same person as the site.

Spend 20 minutes, three days a week, **commenting on posts by ID / LXD / L&D people in Europe** (a useful sentence, not “Great post!”). That is how names stick. Do not dump the portfolio in other people’s comment threads.

### Where the vacancies are

Set alerts. Open them twice a week. Do not live on them.

| Place | Use it for |
|-------|------------|
| LinkedIn Jobs | Main board. Filter: remote, EU, the four titles. |
| The company careers page | The clean apply. Always do this if the advert exists. |
| [Jobgether](https://jobgether.com/), [Remote Rocketship](https://www.remoterocketship.com/), [Working Nomads](https://www.workingnomads.com/) | Extra remote ID / e-learning ads (already on the week-12 list). |
| Specialist e-learning recruiters (UK, Netherlands, Germany) | Search LinkedIn for `e-learning recruiter` + country. One conversation with a real recruiter beats twenty cold emails. |

Do not pay for LinkedIn ads. Do not buy a CIPD membership to “be visible.” Do not buy email lists ([platforms.md](platforms.md) already says skip those).

### Signature

Every mail you send as a **human** (applications, named notes, recruiter replies) ends with:

Keith Byne  
Learning Experience Designer · Seville, EU remote  
[live site URL]  
keith.byne@hotmail.co.uk · [phone once confirmed]

That is how the portfolio sits in email **without** spamming strangers.

## 02 — Be specific (you walk up to the right desk)

Direct approach is allowed. Blanket approach is not.

### Who the right person is

One person, in this order:

1. **The name on the advert** (“Hiring manager: …”, “Reports to Head of Learning Design”).
2. If no name: company LinkedIn → **People** → search `instructional designer`, `learning experience`, `digital learning`, `L&D`. The ID lead or Head of L&D, not the CEO, not `info@`.
3. If the advert gives an apply-to address, use **that** address. That is the legal, human path.

You are looking for companies that **already hire this work**: in-house L&D in EU/UK firms that run remote academies, and e-learning agencies / digital-learning consultancies. Build the list from **live ads this week**, not from a fantasy of “every company.”

### How to write so they actually open it

Six to eight lines. One artefact. One ask. Use the tokens in [cover-letter.md](cover-letter.md) for formal applications. For a LinkedIn note or a short mail:

- Line 1: why **them** (the role, or one sentence on what their team ships).
- Line 2–3: who you are (programme director + ID, 30 years adult learning, Seville, EU remote).
- Line 4: **one** live link (job aid, or Rise once it exists). Not the whole Work page as an attachment.
- Line 5: the ask — “May I send a one-page CV?” or “Would you take 15 minutes if this role is still open?”
- Do not lead with B2B, REPORT-O-MATIC LTD, tax, or “no friction for HR.” They will ask how to hire. Then you answer.

If you cannot name the person, **do not send the mail**. Apply on the careers page instead.

### Volume that a human can keep honest

| Day | You do | Number |
|-----|--------|--------|
| Monday | Read alerts. Shortlist roles that match the four titles and remote EU. | 8 roles on a list |
| Tuesday–Wednesday | Tailored applications. Exact job title. One artefact in paragraph three. | 4 applications |
| Thursday | Direct notes only to **named** people tied to that shortlist. | 4 notes |
| Friday | One polite follow-up on anything 7 days old with no reply. One recruiter or comment session. | 1–2 |

That is about **ten touches a week**. Eight weeks of that is eighty honest approaches. That is a campaign. Two hundred paste-jobs is not.

Keep a simple sheet: date, company, person, role, channel (apply / LinkedIn / mail), artefact you named, next action. Do not invent replies.

### Follow-up

One follow-up, seven days later, three lines. Then stop. Hiring managers notice persistence; they block nagging.

## How you get in front of the right person (the actual moves)

**Move A — Apply like an adult.**  
Open the advert. Apply on the company’s page with the tailored letter. Then, if you have a name, send the six-line note: “I applied for X. Here is one sample of the work.” That pairing is how you skip the ATS pile without being a stranger in the inbox.

**Move B — Recruiter as a multiplier.**  
When a specialist e-learning recruiter accepts a connection, send the same six lines and the live URL. They already have hiring managers. You do not need to guess those inboxes.

**Move C — Be the person who shows the work.**  
Once Rise is live, the Featured link on LinkedIn *is* the notice. People hire from a 15-minute click, not from a manifesto.

**Move D — Direct to a company with no advert.**  
Only if you can name the ID / L&D lead **and** you have a reason (they ship modules, they hire remote EU, you can say one true sentence about their work). Same six lines. No attachment storm. If they ignore you, they have said no.

---

## Do not

- Put the portfolio in “every company email.”
- Buy or scrape addresses.
- Send one letter to 200 `jobs@` inboxes.
- Lead with tax, B2B, or REPORT-O-MATIC LTD.
- Apply as L&D Manager to a pure people-management vacancy in the first wave.
- Claim Rise, Storyline, or SCORM on the CV until the live URL exists ([cv-skills-log.md](cv-skills-log.md)).
- Use a second visual look on PDFs you attach. Same faces and colours as the site.

---

## First week of the attack (checklist)

1. Paste the live URL and phone onto the CV and the letter.
2. LinkedIn: headline, About, Featured, Open to work.
3. Alerts on for the four titles, remote, EU.
4. Sheet created (date / company / person / role / channel / artefact / next).
5. Ten touches, all named, all human.
6. Friday: what got a reply? Do more of that. Drop channels that only produce silence.

The goal is not that every hiring manager in Europe knows your name. The goal is that **the twenty people who can actually hire you this quarter** have seen one piece of work and know how to answer.
