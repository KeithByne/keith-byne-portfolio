# 90-day hybrid onboarding — outline

The shape of the L&D artefact. Not a course. Not the Safe AI module. Not a named employer. The real files come later: a document for the plan, a sheet for the gaps.

## What this is

A plan for one new person’s first 90 days on a hybrid team. Some days with other people, some days remote.

## Needs analysis

The finished page answers three questions.

1. What must they be able to do by day 90?
2. What do they already bring?
3. Which gaps need practice with a person, and which are only information?

## The 90 days

1. **Days 1–30.** Learn the work beside someone. People, tools, and the shape of the week.
2. **Days 31–60.** Do the work with a check. Together when the task is new. A page or a call when it repeats.
3. **Days 61–90.** Own the work. The manager steps back.

How we would notice: the job happens without the manager in the room. No invented percentage.

## Skill-gap sheet

The second file. One row per skill: needed by day 90, where they are now, and how the gap closes — a buddy, practice, one short session, or a page. Not a course catalogue.
