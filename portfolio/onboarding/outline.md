# 90-day hybrid onboarding — outline

The shape of the L&D artefact. Not a course. Not the Safe AI module. Not a named employer. The real files come later: a document for the plan, a sheet for the gaps.

## What this is

A plan for one new operations coordinator on a hybrid team. By day 90 they run the weekly rhythm: handovers, tools, and the meetings that keep the week moving.

## Needs analysis

The finished page answers three questions.

1. What must they be able to do by day 90? Run the weekly rhythm without the manager in the room.
2. What do they already bring?
3. Which gaps need practice with a person, and which are only information?

## Days together

The first week is together. After that, Tuesday and Wednesday are together. Monday, Thursday, and Friday are remote.

## The 90 days

1. **Days 1–30.** Learn the work beside the line manager. People, tools, and the shape of the week. The line manager is the buddy.
2. **Days 31–60.** Do the work with a check. The buddy changes to a peer. Together when the task is new. A page or a call when it repeats.
3. **Days 61–90.** Own the weekly rhythm. The manager steps back. The peer stays available.

How we would notice: handovers, tools, and the weekly meetings happen without the manager in the room. No invented percentage.

## Skill-gap sheet

The second file. One row per skill: needed by day 90, where they are now, and how the gap closes — the line manager in the first 30 days, then the peer, plus practice, one short session, or a page. Not a course catalogue.

## Locked for this sample (24 Sep 2026)

- Role: operations coordinator for a hybrid team.
- Together: first week, then Tuesday and Wednesday. Remote: Monday, Thursday, and Friday.
- Buddy: the line manager for days 1–30, then a peer from day 31.
