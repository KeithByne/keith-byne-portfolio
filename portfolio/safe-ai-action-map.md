# Action map — Safe AI and data handling

Cathy Moore’s four lines, for this portfolio topic. Not a client project. No invented percentage.

| Line | Statement |
|------|-----------|
| **Who** | Knowledge workers on a distributed EU team (office staff, not teachers). |
| **What is going wrong** | People paste client and staff data into public AI tools, and put work files on personal drives so a contractor can “edit overnight.” GDPR and leak risk. |
| **What they must do** | Before any paste, prompt, or file share: name the kind of data (public / internal / personal / customer) → use an approved tool, a stripped example, or no AI → if unsure, stop and ask. |
| **How we would notice** | Fewer unapproved AI pastes and fewer personal-drive shares. We will not invent a percentage. |

**Moment this has to work:** the 30 seconds before they paste.

**What a page can fix:** people who already know the rule but skip it when they are in a hurry. That is the one-page cheat sheet.

**What a short module is for:** people who do not yet stop to name the data. That is the later Rise module (10–15 minutes, one decision, one marked question).

**What the 8 slides are for:** the person who must approve the habit, name who to ask if unsure, and host the page.
