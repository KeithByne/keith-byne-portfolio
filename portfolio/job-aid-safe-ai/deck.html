Problem 2 / 8

The problem is a paste, not a knowledge gap.

Remote staff leak data in two common moves:

  1. “Just drop this into ChatGPT and tidy it.”
  2. “Put it on my personal Drive so the contractor can edit overnight.”

Both are faster than the allowed path. Both create GDPR and leak risk.

You say: Do not invent an incident or a fine. Most companies already have a long policy PDF; nobody opens it at the moment they paste.
