Desk · remote EU team

Before you paste, classify.

Remote work is fast. Public AI and personal drives are faster. Take 30 seconds.

Three checks before any AI or file share

  1. 01What data is this? (public / internal / personal / customer)
  2. 02Is this tool on the approved list?
  3. 03If I am unsure, I do not paste — I ask my line manager or data-protection lead.

Classify

If it is…Treat it as…
Already on the public website, no people namedPublic
Internal process, no personal or customer dataInternal — approved tools only
Names, emails, staff or candidate filesPersonal data — do not paste into public AI
Customer lists, contracts, unpublished commercial filesCustomer / confidential — do not paste into public AI

Then do this

  • Public or already-approved text: approved tool is fine.
  • Personal or customer data: take out the real details or use a fake example, then approved tool — or no AI.
  • Never: paste the live file into a public AI tool; put work files on a personal Drive/Dropbox so a contractor can “just edit overnight.”
If unsure: Stop. Do not paste. Ask your line manager or data-protection lead. → Keep this on the desk
