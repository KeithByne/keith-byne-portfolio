Object Series: Angels in the Architecture

Object Name: bodyBridge

Size: 25x16x13cm.

Materials: clay

Comment: One of a group of maquettes linked to drawings of bridges in Seville. The idea was, and still is, to carve the finished versions in wood, on a larger scale.

Object Series: signs of signs

Object Name: stock

Size: 98x24x14cm.

Materials: wood, metal studs and fabric

Comment: This series of work explores methods of joining materials. These methods were made obvious and as simple as posible. The joining methods soon suggested mediaeval artifacts.

Object Series: Giant hands on the Landscape

Object Name: selection

Size: 64x20x13cm.

Materials: sea-washed stones, wood.

Comment: Made to reflect the arbitrary nature of human selection, whereby we have the human faculty to select one thing from a limitless variety of like things and in our hearts and minds, make that one thing, unique.

Object Series: Giant hands on the Landscape

Object Name: outCropBench

Size: 128x35x35cm.

Materials: wood, cement and shellac.

Comment: To make this object I had to invent a molding process that would allow me to first make the mould and then twist it before casting the final object.
