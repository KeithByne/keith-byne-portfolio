Transferable Skills
  

During my working history I have learned some skills that I think can help me in the field of web design. Here is a list of the more obvious ones.

 
From teaching English:
Controlling group dynamics.
Presentation techniques.
Inter-personal skills
Dealing with people of all professional levels.
Listening.
Negotiating.
Language levelling

(Tailoring the communication to the linguistic limitations of the interlocutor).

 
Analysis of:
Client's needs
Communication
Language use
Non-verbal communication

Anticipating possible misunderstanding.

 
From Rank Zerox Sales Training:
A professional presentation
Developing an environment of agreement
Developing needs
Presenting features
Demonstration and explanation
Overcoming objections
Justifying actions/costs

Closing a negotiation with commitment

 
From Sculpture:
A practical, problem solving approach to challenges.
Mentally visualising from plans in three dimensions.
Considering concepts/arguments "In the Round" from all sides.

Tailoring products to needs (Form follows Function) while not forgetting possible future requirements.

 
From Graphic Design:
Some relevant terminology (Kerning, Leading and so on).
An understanding of the inherent values of type faces (What they suggest).
A general awareness of the fashions in layout and graphic design.
Tailoring the layout according to the limitations of the means of production.
Celebrating those limitations and using them to influence other forms of production.
Working within and/or managing a production team.
Developing a creative environment for design and production.
Understanding deadlines and
giving accurate feedback to the production director about completion times.
Visualising client's ideas and
helping the client to recognise the limitations of the production technology.
