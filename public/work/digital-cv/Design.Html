Design

In terms of design I have worked in many different areas from bussiness cards to signage on aeroplanes.

 

Print

I have designed for silk screen, lithographic and even wood-cut printing. Most of the work I have done has been on a reasonably large scale and has been used with vinyl lettering for vehicles. The limitations of vinyl make it necessary, at times, to print the vinyl before application. Also in the handling of corporate design I have designed all types of office stationary.

Interior

My first job was to manufacture interior signs for large corporations in and around London. This was mostly production work following corporate mandates but later I was given the chance to control the appearance also. In this role I have worked with some well known companies such as Tailor Woodrow Construction.

Exterior

A natural progression from the interior work was to work on the outside of the buildings with the addition of the consideration of the elements and vandalism. This was the lasrgest work I have done to date at times designing for an area of thirty meters square.

Vehicles

I have worked with many kinds of vehicles, indeed it was the main part of my work. I will admit to prefering to work on the more romantic vehicles like aeroplanes, racing cars and helicopters. The work has its challenges in the fact that cars, busses and jets are not symetrical.

Logos

Corporate design such as company logos was always part of the work I have done. With the ability to build close working relationships with the clients, the work was very rewarding. I particularly liked working with small companies with one director. It is not hard to imagine why.

Web

Until now my professional experience in web design is limited to "helping out". The interest I have had in web work goes back years but I have never been in a position to look for work in this field for reasons of other commitments or language. I have however "helped out" in a few web sites in England and France. In England with small businesses and in France with Eurosport and Telecom France. The envolvement was in the main to consider navigation strategies for large sites and the effect on the page layout. I am also working with a group of professors from Westminster University in developement of E-Learning facilities on the web.
