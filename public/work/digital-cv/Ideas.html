Ideas on the Web

Jazzy Pages

Many pages are loaded with "wizardry" that takes an age to load and is not worth the waiting.

Many web sites are limited in what they offer the reader and the company itself. Routines can be developed to help customers define their needs to the company and then this information can be used by companies to develop their product ranges.

Solid visual design is more effective than dancing words. Animation is a powerful tool in the right place. Icons communicate meaning and help pages become more stimulating, less crowded and non-lingual, intuitive and faster.

E-Learning

Infranets are the way forward for government prescribed education for schools.

They have all the advantages of the internet, without the obvious hazards.

Education and trainning can be developed, packaged, sold, installed, run and upgraded.

Markets

The publishing problem of obsolescence will force web development.

In-house training for companies is a market that is developing within large companies.

The private education sector is a developing area.

The problem is marketing something freely available on the web.

Markets are schools and companies not the internaughts.

Institutions

Offer the visitor pre-visit pages. To develop interest, test awareness and collect information.

Post visit pages offer activities and further information to make "a day out" into a learning experience.

The establishment will gather information about how the location was used by the visitor.

Using such information will make changes where needed.

Web Companies

Large Web Companies with R&D. departments lead the way.

Dominate design in their countries and often in other countries that share the same language.

Contract smaller creative companies to handle parts of large sites.

Add diversity of style and presentation and share the workload.
