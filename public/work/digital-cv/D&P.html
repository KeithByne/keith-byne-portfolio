Production

Computers

My work has always been linked with the use of computers. The sign industry was one of the big markets for the computers with a graphic application. The change from 'paint and ladder' to 'computer and ladder' took about four years. These were the first four years of my working life. I was employed to operate a CAD/CAM system for creating signs from vinyl, perspex, wood and metals.

 

Vector Graphics

The machines advanced rapidly and it was not long before it was posible to create layouts instead of single line text. It did not have a monitor. The calculations were all done in the head of the operator and if you could visualise well, you could save a grate deal of operation time. Later still the ability to manually 'digitise' images was realised. These early vector based images had to be quite simple.

 

The PC

Finally to complete the picture, the PC was introduced and the whole process was made much faster and for the first time the client could see a representation of the design. At this time I was designing for vehicles, signs, information boards, airoplanes, helicopters and even the occasional off-shore oil rig.

 

Print

New businesses wanted a more complete service than the sign alone. Not wanting to turn away business I learned about the various forms of printing. I became able to consider all the production means involved in the meeting of our customer's needs. I also consider from an early point the eventual aim of my designs and overcome the problems before they arise.

 

Team Work

My job developed from working alone to managing a small production department consisting of ten people. It was my job to consider every aspect of the process and make it efficient. It was also my responsibility to manage the various different personalities that made up that team. The emphasis was always on creativity in every aspect of the job. The motto "Anyone can have a good idea" was understood and used.
