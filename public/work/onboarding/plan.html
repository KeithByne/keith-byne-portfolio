90-day onboarding — portfolio sample

Run the week.

One new operations coordinator. By day 90 they run handovers, tools, and the meetings that keep the week moving.

Days together

First week. Together. The line manager is the buddy.

After that. Tuesday and Wednesday together. Monday, Thursday, and Friday remote.

The 90 days

01

Days 1–30

Learn the work beside the line manager. People, tools, and the shape of the week.

02

Days 31–60

A peer becomes the buddy. Do the work with a check. Together when the task is new. A page or a call when it repeats.

03

Days 61–90

Own the weekly rhythm. The manager steps back. The peer stays available.

By day 90 — and what this is not

By day 90

  • Handovers go out without a rewrite.
  • The shared tools are current.
  • The weekly meetings happen.

Not this

  • A course catalogue.
  • A named employer.
  • An invented percentage.
