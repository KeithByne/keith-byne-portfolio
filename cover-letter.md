## How to tailor (do not send this section)

- Use the exact `[Role title]` from the advert.
- Do not lead with tax, B2B, or “no friction for HR.”
- Match emphasis to the advert: operations (Universal English), materials (Teachertrainingvideos.com / BBC / China), corporate delivery (Paris ESP), or visual production (design years).
- Microsoft appears twice in the source CV: **design accounts (1984–1996)** and **Paris ESP (2000–2002)**. Do not merge them into one “Microsoft L&D” story.
- If no artefact exists yet, delete the last sentence of the third paragraph.
