# December interview plan

Active looking starts **Monday 1 December 2026**, and only if the Rise 360 and Storyline 360 modules are on the live site. October and November stay on the portfolio. You may open accounts and alerts before December. You may not send a pile of applications before the gate below is true.

**The best option:** four job titles, two places you are visible, and about ten honest touches a week. The employer table is a watch list of companies that already do this work. It is not a mail-merge, and a page that existed on 4 October 2026 is not a promise of a vacancy in December.

**How a hiring manager will read a Europe-wide blast:** spam. In the European Union (EU) it also collides with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Write to a named person about a named role, with one live artefact. The writing rules stay in [outreach-plan.md](outreach-plan.md). Pay bands stay in [how-industry-works.md](how-industry-works.md). Do not lead with tax, autónomo, or REPORT-O-MATIC LTD.

Default package: employee on a monthly salary, fully remote. Contractor invoice only when the advert asks. Days in an office are not a search. On 4 October 2026 no employer in Seville, Córdoba, or Huelva met the rung-1 titles and the languages on the CV. ATEXIS in Seville does post instructional design, and sometimes days in that office, but the advert requires French at B2. French is not on the CV.

## The ladder

December applications use rung 1 only. Rung 2 waits until a Storyline module has a public address. Rung 3 is later, and only when the advert is still a design job.

| Rung | When | Titles to search |
|------|------|------------------|
| **1 — the door** | December 2026 | Instructional Designer. eLearning Developer. Learning Experience Designer (LXD). Digital Learning Designer. |
| **2 — the next seat** | After the Storyline address is live, and the advert names senior work or Storyline | Senior Instructional Designer. Learning Designer or Learning Experience Consultant in an agency, when the advert is design, not sales. |
| **3 — later** | Not the first wave | Learning and development (L&D) Manager, or Digital Learning Manager, only where the advert is design-heavy or player-coach. |

Do not apply in December for a pure people-management vacancy, a learning-platform administrator seat, or a learning-analyst seat. Those are other jobs. Native English is the language on the CV. Do not claim French, German, or Dutch. Spanish for interviews is still `[TO CONFIRM]` in [open-questions.md](open-questions.md). If an advert requires another language, skip it.

## Stage 03 — From 8 December: ten touches a week

Open the alerts twice a week. Do not live on the boards.

| Day | Step | Number |
|-----|------|--------|
| Monday | Read alerts. Keep roles that match rung 1 and are fully remote in the EU. Drop the rest. | 8 roles on the log |
| Tuesday and Wednesday | Apply on the company careers page. Exact job title. One artefact in the letter. | 4 applications |
| Thursday | A short note only to a **named** person tied to one of those applications. If you cannot name them, do not send the note. | 4 notes |
| Friday | One follow-up on anything seven days old with no reply. One recruiter conversation or one comment session. | 1 or 2 |

That is about ten touches. One follow-up, then stop.

Who the named person is, in order: the name on the advert; if there is none, the company’s LinkedIn People search for instructional designer, learning experience, digital learning, or L&D; the apply-to address on the advert if that is all they give. Not the chief executive. Not `info@`.

A specialist e-learning recruiter is a multiplier, not a list to buy. When one accepts a connection, send the same six lines and the live address. Search LinkedIn for `e-learning recruiter` plus the country. Instinct’s public salary guide is already cited in [how-industry-works.md](how-industry-works.md). That does not make Instinct a promised introduction.

**Done when:** the log has ten real rows that week, each with a next action.

## Stage 04 — When someone answers: the interview

1. Reply in one working day. Offer two times. Ask for thirty minutes if they do not name a length.
2. Have one artefact ready to share on screen. The Rise module once it is live. Not eight files.
3. Be able to say, in one minute, who the learner is, what they must do differently, and how you would know. That is the Action Mapping story from the portfolio.
4. If they ask how to hire you: employee on a monthly salary is the default. Spanish B2B / autónomo, or REPORT-O-MATIC LTD, only if they ask. EU work is already authorised. No visa sponsorship.
5. If they ask about tools: name only what is live. Rise and Storyline after the addresses exist. Canva and PowerPoint are already on the CV. Do not claim a Learning Management System (LMS) you have not run. The honest line is Google Classroom at academy scale, and you will learn theirs.
6. After the call, write the next action on the log the same day. One thank-you note, three lines, the same day. Then wait.

**Done when:** the conversation has a next step written down, or a clear no.
