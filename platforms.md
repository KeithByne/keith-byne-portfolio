# Industry platforms (hiring order)

Learn in this order. A live artefact beats a certificate.

## Must show (weeks 1–8)

| Platform | Why ads ask for it | How to get it without spending | When |
|----------|--------------------|--------------------------------|------|
| Cathy Moore Action Mapping | Shows training is for a business behaviour, not a content dump | Free articles on Cathy Moore’s site | Week 1 |
| Canva | Job aids and visual assets | Free tier | Week 2 |
| PowerPoint | Stakeholder decks; still named in ID ads | Existing Office / free web | Week 2 |
| Articulate Rise 360 | Responsive corporate modules | 30-day Articulate 360 trial, no card. **Do not start until the storyboard is written.** | Weeks 5–6 |
| Articulate Storyline 360 | Hard filter on Senior ID ads (states, variables, branching) | Same trial | Weeks 7–8 |
| SCORM 1.2 | Proof the module will load in an LMS | Export from Articulate | Week 8 |
| SCORM Cloud | Live tracked demo for employers | Free account | Weeks 4 and 8 |
| Netlify | Host HTML5 export after the trial dies | Free tier. Do not rely on Review 360 links. | Week 8 |

## Should show (visual advantage)

| Platform | Why | How | When |
|----------|-----|-----|------|
| Photoshop / Illustrator | Fine Art background; ads still name Adobe | Use if already installed; do not buy CC for this course | As needed for assets |
| Figma | Wireframes / LXD layout | Free | Week 3 storyboard |
| DaVinci Resolve or CapCut | One process video | Free | Week 11 optional |

## Touch once — do not claim expertise

- One LMS upload: the product Keith already runs, or SCORM Cloud if that name is still unconfirmed.
- Names to recognise in ads only: Workday Learning, Docebo, 360Learning, LearnUpon, Rise Up.
- One short AI clip (Synthesia or HeyGen trial) as a supporting asset, not the hero.

## Skip for 90 days

Paid CIPD, a paid ID master’s, TalentLMS as a billed product, Coursebox, Vyond, purchased email lists.
